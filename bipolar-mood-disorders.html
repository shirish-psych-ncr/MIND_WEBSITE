Mood concerns

A careful assessment for changing mood

Mood shifts, periods of low mood, increased energy, reduced sleep, impulsivity, or irritability deserve careful context rather than quick conclusions.

Ask about an assessmentPsychiatry consultations

Questions a clinician may consider

Timeline

Changes in sleep, energy, activity, mood, concentration, and functioning over time.

Context

Health, medicines, substances, stressors, family history, and previous episodes may be relevant.

Plan

Care may include follow-up, medication discussion, therapy, practical support, or referral.

Safety: If someone is at immediate risk, call 112 or attend the nearest emergency department.
