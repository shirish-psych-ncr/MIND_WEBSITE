Remote care enquiry

Ask whether teleconsultation is suitable

Some follow-up or counselling conversations may be possible remotely, but teleconsultation is not suitable for every person, concern, or situation.

Ask the clinicRequest an appointment

Before a remote appointment

Eligibility

The clinic must confirm clinician availability, location, identity, consent, privacy, and whether remote care is clinically appropriate.

Privacy

Use a private room and a reliable connection. Share only the information requested through the clinic’s approved booking channel.

Limits

Remote care may require an in-person review, examination, testing, referral, or local emergency support.

Emergency warning: Do not use teleconsultation, email, WhatsApp, or this website for immediate danger or crisis support. Call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Consent and follow-up

Ask how consent, prescriptions, records, fees, cancellations, and follow-up will work before the consultation. Read the clinic’s privacy information.
