Adult psychiatry

Psychiatry consultations that start with listening

A psychiatric consultation can help clarify what you are experiencing, what may be contributing to it, and which next steps could be appropriate.

Request a consultationSee what to expect

What may be discussed

Assessment

Symptoms, history, sleep, physical health, medicines, substance use, relationships, and daily functioning may be reviewed.

Medication management

If medication is relevant, the clinician can discuss options, benefits, risks, monitoring, and follow-up.

Practical care planning

Recommendations may include counselling, lifestyle steps, further assessment, referral, or review over time.

Important: A webpage cannot diagnose or prescribe. Suitability, diagnosis, medication, and referrals are decided during a clinical consultation.
