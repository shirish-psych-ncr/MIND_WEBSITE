Learning and school concerns

Understand learning differences beyond a test score

Learning disability testing is most useful when it connects assessment findings with the child’s classroom experience, development, strengths, and support needs.

Assessment informationChild development

What may be reviewed

School experience

Reading, writing, maths, attention, homework, classroom feedback, and learning history may be discussed.

Wider development

Language, cognition, health, sleep, emotions, behaviour, and developmental history provide important context.

Useful recommendations

Recommendations may support home, school, further assessment, or referral where appropriate.

Assessment scope: Tools, sessions, and reports depend on the referral question and are confirmed before booking.
