Emotional support

There is room to talk about loss and difficult experiences

Grief, trauma, and adjustment can affect sleep, mood, relationships, concentration, and the body. Support begins with a conversation at a pace that feels manageable.

Making sense of change

Talk through what happened, what has changed, and how it is affecting daily life.

Building steadiness

Support may include grounding, routines, coping skills, sleep support, and relationship resources.

Knowing when to refer

Severe symptoms, immediate danger, or specialist needs may require another service or urgent care.
