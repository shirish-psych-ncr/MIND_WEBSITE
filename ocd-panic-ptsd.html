Anxiety and trauma-related concerns

When thoughts, fear, or memories take over

Intrusive thoughts, compulsions, panic, avoidance, nightmares, or trauma-related distress can be exhausting. A confidential assessment can clarify possible next steps.

Request supportExplore therapy

Support is individual

Understand the pattern

The clinician may explore triggers, thoughts, behaviours, physical sensations, safety, and impact on daily life.

Discuss options

Options may include therapy, coping strategies, medication discussion, or referral depending on the assessment.

Move at a safe pace

Care planning should respect readiness, privacy, culture, relationships, and current safety.
