Questions people ask before a first visit

Common questions about confidentiality, treatment approach, costs, and what to expect during your appointment at Mind Grace in Gamma II, Greater Noida.

Direct answer: Mind Grace Neuropsychiatric Clinic in Greater Noida answers the questions people ask before a first visit: consultations are confidential within professional and legal limits, medication is only one option among several, fees are listed on the fees page, and you can book by phone or online form, including teleconsultations.

Confidentiality

Consultations are handled confidentially within professional and legal boundaries. Limits can apply when there is an immediate safety risk or when disclosure is required by law. Ask the clinic if you want to understand how a specific situation is handled.

Access is limited to the clinician and staff who need information for care or appointment administration. Information may also be shared where you have consented or where disclosure is required by law.

Medication and Treatment

Not necessarily. Medication is considered when clinically appropriate, but we also offer practical strategies, behavioural interventions, and therapeutic options. The treatment plan is personalized based on your specific needs, preferences, and clinical assessment.

The clinic offers psychiatric consultations and counselling support. Depending on your needs, the clinician may discuss therapy, practical strategies, medication, or referral to another professional.

Booking and Logistics

Use the booking form, call +91 96678 63295, or send a WhatsApp message. The clinic aims to respond within 24–48 hours using the contact method you provide; this is not an emergency service.

Fees can vary by consultation type. Please check the fees page for the current information or contact the clinic before booking if you need clarification.

Please confirm coverage directly with your insurer before your visit. Ask the clinic whether the available receipt or documentation can support your particular claim; reimbursement depends on your policy.
