Psychiatric assessment

Support when reality feels difficult to make sense of

Changes in perception, beliefs, thinking, behaviour, sleep, or functioning need a timely and careful clinical assessment, often with family context.

Contact the clinicUrgent-help information

What may be considered

Current symptoms

The clinician may discuss what has changed, how quickly, sleep, health, medicines, substances, and daily safety.

Family involvement

With consent and where appropriate, family observations can help build a clearer picture and support plan.

Referral needs

Some situations need urgent hospital assessment or coordinated specialist care beyond an outpatient clinic.

Urgent safety: If there is immediate danger, severe confusion, or risk of harm, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.
