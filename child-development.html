Children, adolescents, and families

Child-development care that includes the family

Families can seek guidance for communication, learning, attention, behaviour, sensory experiences, or developmental differences. The first step is understanding the child’s context.

Request a family appointmentRead about Aasha

What families may explore

Development and communication

Concerns about speech, language, social communication, play, or early milestones can be discussed.

Attention and learning

School concerns, ADHD questions, learning differences, and practical classroom or home strategies may be explored.

Behaviour and regulation

Families can talk about routines, emotional regulation, sensory needs, sleep, behaviour, and parenting support.

Assessment note: An online checklist cannot diagnose autism, ADHD, or a learning disability. A clinician decides what assessment or referral is appropriate.
