Therapy and psychotherapy

Therapy is a collaborative conversation

Therapy may help you notice patterns, build coping skills, work through relationships or experiences, and make practical changes over time.

Ask about therapyPsychology and counselling

Formats and approaches

Individual therapy

One-to-one work may address anxiety, depression, stress, grief, trauma, confidence, sleep, or adjustment.

Couples and family work

Relationship, marriage, parenting, and family counselling may be considered when shared conversations are useful.

Evidence-informed skills

CBT-informed, behavioural, supportive, mindfulness, and other approaches may be discussed according to fit.

Availability note: The clinic does not promise a particular modality, therapist, duration, or result. A suitable plan is discussed after understanding your needs.
