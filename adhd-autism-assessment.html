Neurodevelopmental assessment

ADHD and autism questions deserve context

Assessment considers development, communication, attention, behaviour, learning, sensory experiences, health, and everyday functioning across settings.

Assessment informationChild development

What an assessment may include

History and observation

Developmental history, family observations, school feedback, and the person’s own experience may be gathered.

Structured tools

Questionnaires or tests may be used when clinically appropriate; no single checklist gives a diagnosis.

Recommendations

The outcome may include practical strategies, school guidance, follow-up, or referral.

Important: Online content cannot diagnose ADHD or autism. Assessment scope, duration, and report format are confirmed before booking.
