Your First Visit

Know what to expect before you visit.

You do not need a prior diagnosis to request an appointment. We explain the next step, what to bring, and how follow-up is planned.

Request an appointment

Use the form or contact the clinic by phone or WhatsApp. Share basic contact details and a short description of what you need help with.

Have a focused first visit

Your first session is a structured conversation with Dr. Sharma about your history, current concerns, daily functioning, and goals.

Discuss next steps

You will have time to discuss options, which may include treatment, counselling, a prescription, or further assessment where appropriate.
