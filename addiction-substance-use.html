Addiction support starts with the right level of care

Substance use, behavioural addiction, withdrawal risk, and dual-diagnosis concerns can need different services. The first step is an honest, non-judgemental assessment.

Specialized enquiriesContact the clinic

What to clarify

Safety and urgency

Withdrawal, overdose risk, severe symptoms, or immediate danger may require emergency or hospital care.

Support needs

Assessment may consider substances, pattern, health, mental health, family support, and readiness for change.

Referral options

Specialist addiction treatment, rehabilitation, medical care, or community support may be recommended.

Qualified availability: This page does not promise addiction treatment or rehabilitation at Mind Grace. Suitability and referral are assessed case by case.
