Common mental-health concerns

Support for depression, anxiety, and panic

Persistent worry, low mood, panic, irritability, poor sleep, or loss of interest can affect everyday life. A consultation can help clarify what is happening and what support may fit.

Request a consultationPsychiatry pathway

What may be explored

Your pattern

Onset, duration, triggers, physical symptoms, sleep, functioning, and previous support may be discussed.

Treatment options

Depending on the assessment, options may include therapy, practical strategies, medication discussion, or referral.

Ongoing support

Follow-up and adjustment are guided by response, preferences, safety, and clinical judgement.

Not a diagnosis: Similar symptoms can have different causes. A clinician must assess you before recommending treatment.
