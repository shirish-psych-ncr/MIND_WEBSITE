Assessment and evaluation

Assessment that answers a useful question

Mind Grace Neuropsychiatric Clinic in Greater Noida provides psychiatric evaluation, psychological testing, and child and learning assessments for adults, children, and families. An assessment is most helpful when it begins with a clear concern and ends with understandable recommendations—not just a label or a score.

Assessment pathways

Psychiatric evaluation

A clinician reviews symptoms, history, health, and functioning to guide diagnosis and next steps.

Psychological assessment

Structured interviews or tests may help understand cognition, behaviour, emotions, or learning.

Child and learning assessment

ADHD, autism, developmental, learning, and school concerns are considered in the child’s wider context.

Scope note: The assessment plan, tools, number of sessions, and report format depend on the referral question and clinical judgement.
