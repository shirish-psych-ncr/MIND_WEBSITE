Specialized concerns

Sleep and eating concerns need careful context

Insomnia, irregular sleep, appetite changes, restrictive eating, bingeing, or distress about food can have overlapping physical and mental-health causes.

Assessment may consider

Daily pattern

Sleep timing, food patterns, distress, routines, work or school, and physical symptoms may be discussed.

Health context

Medicines, medical conditions, substances, mood, anxiety, and energy levels can matter.

Specialist support

Some eating or sleep concerns need a physician, dietitian, sleep service, or specialist team.
