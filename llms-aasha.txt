### My child lost words or skills they used before. Is that urgent?

Loss of an established skill deserves prompt medical discussion—contact your paediatrician or Aasha and say explicitly that skills have been lost. If a child is difficult to wake, has breathing difficulty, sudden weakness or a prolonged seizure, seek emergency care immediately rather than waiting for a developmental appointment.

### Does Aasha recommend special diets, supplements or biomedical protocols for autism?

No. Aasha focuses on assessment, therapy and coordinated medical care. Elimination diets, supplement stacks or tests marketed as autism treatments should be discussed with your clinician first; restriction without a specific medical reason can harm nutrition, and unexplained worsening should be assessed rather than called detoxification.

### Can my child start therapy before receiving a diagnosis?

Often yes. Support planning can begin from observed needs—communication, sensory comfort, learning or regulation—while assessment continues. A diagnosis may help explain needs and access services, but families do not need to wait for one to start a practical conversation about support.
