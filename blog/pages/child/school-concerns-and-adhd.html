When school concerns may point toward ADHD

A teacher’s note about distraction or incomplete work can leave families unsure what to do next. This guide helps you compare patterns across settings and prepare useful questions.

Questions worth asking

  • Is the difficulty happening across settings, not just in one class?
  • Is there restlessness, forgetfulness, or task avoidance?
  • Is school confidence starting to drop?
