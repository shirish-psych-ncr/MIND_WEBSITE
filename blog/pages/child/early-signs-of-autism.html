Early signs of autism parents often ask about

Parents often begin with practical observations about eye contact, name response, interaction, play, or repetition. This brief note introduces patterns to discuss with a qualified professional, without treating one sign as a diagnosis.

What to notice early

No single sign confirms anything on its own, but recurring patterns across communication, interaction, and behavior may justify assessment.
