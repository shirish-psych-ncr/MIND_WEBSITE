When to see a psychiatrist

You do not have to wait for symptoms to become unbearable before asking for help. If mood, worry, sleep, or daily functioning is being affected, an evaluation can bring useful clarity.

Useful threshold questions

  • Is sleep, work, or routine being disrupted?
  • Are you stuck in the same pattern despite trying to cope?
  • Do you need diagnostic clarity or medication guidance?
