Overthinking vs anxiety

When thoughts keep circling, it can be hard to tell whether you are dealing with a thinking habit, anxiety, or both. This guide helps you notice the difference and choose a useful next step.

A useful distinction

Overthinking is often the mental pattern. Anxiety is the wider state that may also affect your body, sleep, and sense of safety.
