From the IILM classroom to clinical practice

Your psychology degree builds a foundation in human behaviour, research and psychological assessment. IILM's official undergraduate psychology programme page describes BA and BSc pathways and additional training in SPSS, ATLAS and NVivo. Programme availability and learning opportunities vary by campus and course, so check your own syllabus before planning an internship.

Textbooks explain diagnostic criteria. Classroom exercises introduce interviewing and therapeutic techniques. Clinical exposure adds another dimension: understanding how professionals build rapport, approach an assessment, document observations and protect patient confidentiality.

For a BA (Hons) or BSc (Hons) Psychology student, a useful internship should turn those ideas into supervised learning. Choose a placement by the quality of its mentorship, the clarity of your role and its fit with your university requirements.

The traditional route: psychology internships across Delhi NCR

Hospitals, specialist clinics, NGOs and research organisations offer different learning environments. The institutions below are starting points for enquiries, not a list of confirmed undergraduate vacancies. Ask each department about current eligibility, dates, fees, supervision and the exact location before applying.

Start with your IILM internship coordinator too. Existing university placement routes may help you identify a setting that meets your course's required hours, assessment format and documentation.

What to check before choosing a clinical internship

A large hospital can provide valuable exposure to multidisciplinary care. A smaller clinic may offer more time for individual discussion. Neither the name nor the size of the institution tells you how much structured learning you will receive.

  1. Mentor access: Who supervises you, how often do you meet, and how many students share that time? Ask specifically about individual feedback and case discussions.
  2. Your learning role: Will you observe, practise with simulated cases, review anonymized material or assist with permitted tasks? Ask for a learning plan appropriate to your year of study.
  3. University paperwork: Confirm the NOC or referral-letter requirements, attendance records, logbook sign-off and whether IILM will accept the placement.
  4. Schedule and travel: Check daily hours, examination flexibility and realistic travel time before committing.
  5. Costs and outcomes: Request written details of any fee, refund terms, duration, completion criteria and certificate or recommendation arrangements.

Observation is a legitimate part of undergraduate clinical learning. Its value increases when you can discuss what you observed with a qualified supervisor.

4. Skills you can build on

Actionable tips for IILM internship applicants

  1. Prepare a focused CV. Include your degree, campus, semester, relevant modules, research projects and actual experience with assessment or analysis software. Describe your current skills accurately.
  2. Bring your university requirements. Ask your coordinator about minimum hours, NOC or request letters, attendance and the final report. Obtain approval before relying on the internship for academic credit.
  3. State your availability. Include preferred dates, weekly hours and examination commitments. Apply ahead of your intended break so there is time to arrange supervision.
  4. Define what you want to learn. Choose two or three goals, such as writing a structured observation note, understanding an assessment workflow or discussing a case formulation.
  5. Confirm the practical details. Before joining, agree on the duration, schedule, any fees, documentation, supervisor and completion expectations.

Mind Grace accepts rolling applications based on mentor availability. A submitted form is an expression of interest, not confirmation of a placement.

Frequently asked questions

Can IILM BA and BSc Psychology students apply?

Yes. This programme is designed for undergraduate psychology students. Include your campus, course, year, preferred dates and university requirements so the clinic can assess a suitable learning plan.

Will I get hands-on clinical experience?

Learning may include supervised exercises, case discussions and consented observation. Access to particular tests, sessions or tasks depends on patient consent, your training level and supervisor approval. Independent clinical practice is not part of the internship.

What are the fees, duration and application deadline?

Applications are considered on a rolling basis, subject to supervision capacity. Contact the clinic to confirm current fees, duration, hours and the next available start date before making arrangements.

Will IILM count this internship toward my degree?

Your university decides whether a placement meets its academic requirements. Ask your IILM coordinator to approve the proposed learning plan, hours and documentation before you join.

Where is Mind Grace, and how do I apply?

The clinic is in Gamma II, Greater Noida. Complete the internship application form with your academic background and availability.
