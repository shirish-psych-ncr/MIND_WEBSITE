Possible areas of support

Individual work

Support may focus on anxiety, low mood, stress, confidence, grief, trauma, sleep, or coping with change.

Relationships and family

Family counselling, couples counselling, parenting support, and communication work may be considered where appropriate.

Skills and approaches

Depending on the clinician and concern, work may include CBT-informed, behavioural, supportive, mindfulness, or other approaches.

Availability note: A specific therapist, modality, session format, or outcome is not guaranteed. The clinic will discuss suitability before care begins.
