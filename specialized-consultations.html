Topics that require individual confirmation

Addiction and rehabilitation

Addiction, substance use, dual-diagnosis, and rehabilitation needs may require a coordinated or specialist service.

Eating and gender-related concerns

Eating-disorder or gender-related support should be approached respectfully and may require specialist assessment or referral.

Forensic and legal matters

Forensic assessment, court-ordered therapy, and legal reports have specific requirements and are not assumed to be available.

Qualified availability: This page is informational. Availability, suitability, clinician scope, and referral options are confirmed case by case.

Urgent or immediate risk

Mind Grace is not an emergency, crisis-intervention, or suicide-prevention service. If there is immediate danger, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. See emergency resources.
